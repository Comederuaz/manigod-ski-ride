RENTING A SNOWBOARD AT LA CROIX-FRY

Manigod Ski Ride rents snowboards and boots at the Col de la Croix-Fry, on the La Clusaz–Manigod ski area. The shop is right at the foot of the slopes: pick up your gear and you're on the snow. Book online: 10% off with code WINTER27 for any booking made before 19 December.
