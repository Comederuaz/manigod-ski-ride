CROSS-COUNTRY SKIING IN MANIGOD

From the shop at the Col de la Croix-Fry, you reach the Beauregard plateau Nordic area: trails through the forest and across open meadows, facing the Aravis. We kit you out for classic or skating, adults and kids. Book online: 10% off with code WINTER27 for any booking made before 19 December.

[ 01 ]CLASSIC OR SKATING?Classic is skied in the tracks with a diagonal stride: ideal to discover the sport and enjoy the scenery. Skating, on the groomed trail, is faster and more physical.
[ 02 ]FOR KIDSJunior skis, boots and poles, for classic and skating.
[ 03 ]MIX YOUR ACTIVITIESStaying in Manigod? Alternate alpine skiing, cross-country and snowshoeing: ask us in the shop.
