[ WHY MANIGOD SKI RIDE ]

OUR PASSION

In love with skiing and the mountains, we chose to make a living from that passion and share it with everyone. From alpine skiing to ski touring, from the child earning a first medal to the beginner discovering the snow, all the way to the elite skier chasing performance.
