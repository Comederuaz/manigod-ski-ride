FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Ski rental at the Col de la Croix-Fry: booking, pick-up, kids, lockers, workshop. Can't find your answer? Call us on +33 4 50 44 91 39.

[ BOOKING ]

How do I book my gear?

Online on this site by choosing your dates and activity. You can also book by phone on +33 4 50 44 91 39 or come straight to the shop at the Col de la Croix-Fry.

Is there a discount for booking online?

Yes: −10% with the code WINTER27 for online bookings made before 15 December. See our good deals.

Can I rent without booking?

Yes, we have gear available on the spot. During school holidays, booking is the best way to get your size and model.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Bookings cannot be cancelled or refunded, except in cases of force majeure. To change your dates, call us on +33 4 50 44 91 39.

[ PICK-UP AND RETURN ]

Where do I pick up my gear?

At the shop at the Col de la Croix-Fry, right on the slopes, opposite the ESF and the lift-pass office. Large free car park in front of the shop.

Can I pick up the day before?

Yes, you can pick up your gear the day before, during shop opening hours.

What are your opening hours?

Every day 8.30am – 6pm, and until 10pm on night-skiing evenings.

What if a boot hurts?

Come back to the shop: we adjust or swap during your stay. That's the perk of being right on the slopes.

[ GEAR AND KIDS ]

What gear do you rent?

Alpine skis, snowboards, touring skis with safety kit, classic and skating cross-country skis, snowshoes and sledges. See rental.

Do you rent kids' gear?

Yes, kids' skis and boots. No minimum age: we kit out kids from their very first day on snow, helmet included if needed.

Can I buy the gear I rented?

Yes, the skis and boots you rent can be bought. Just ask us in the shop.

Do you offer damage and theft cover?

Yes, available in the shop when you pick up your gear: €1 per day.

[ SERVICES ]

Do you have ski lockers?

Yes, heated lockers just above the shop. €13 per day, €68.60 for 6 days. One locker fits 4 people.

Do you service skis?

Yes, in our workshop: edges, wax, repairs and binding mounting.

Do you do bootfitting?

Yes, at the shop and at Versant Nord in Thônes. See bootfitting.

Payment and deposit?

Payment is made online when you book. In the shop, we accept card, cash and chèques-vacances.
