TERMS AND CONDITIONS

General rental terms of Manigod Ski Ride (HAUTE-SAVOIE SPORTS SAS, 7066 route du Col de la Croix-Fry, 74230 Manigod, France). They apply to every rental, in the shop or booked online.

Online booking

An online booking holds your gear for the dates you choose. The rental contract is drawn up in the shop when you pick up your gear.

Payment is made online when you book. In the shop, we accept card, cash and chèques-vacances.

You can pick up your gear the day before, during shop opening hours. This early pick-up is not charged.

Cancellation and changes

Bookings cannot be cancelled or refunded, except in cases of force majeure. To change your dates, call us on +33 4 50 44 91 39 or email info@manigod-ski-ride.com.

Under Article L221-28 12° of the French Consumer Code, the right of withdrawal does not apply to leisure equipment rental provided on a specific date.

Damage and theft cover

The damage and theft cover is optional. It can be added when you book online or in the shop when you pick up your gear, and cannot be added afterwards.

Price. €1 per day per item, €2 per day for Diamond, Stöckli and Van Deer skis. One item = one pair of skis (alpine, cross-country or touring) or one snowboard, with its poles. Boots and helmets are not covered. The price of the cover is never refunded.

Damage and theft. In the event of accidental damage to or theft of covered equipment, the customer is released from the repair or replacement costs set out in Articles 1 and 2, up to €600 per item and after paying an excess per item: €25 for child and junior skis and snowboards; €40 for adult Blue, Red and Black skis, adult snowboards and adult cross-country skis; €75 for Diamond skis; €100 for touring skis; €400 for Stöckli and Van Deer skis; €2 for poles. Only one claim per item is covered during the rental.

Exclusions. Not covered: loss or disappearance of the equipment; theft between 6 pm and 9 am, unless there was a break-in or the equipment was left in the shop's ski locker; cosmetic damage (scratches, chips) that does not prevent use; damage caused by not following the shop's instructions for use and care; intentional fault or fraud.

Making a claim. Inform the shop within 5 working days, or within 2 working days for theft. For damage, bring the damaged equipment back to the shop. For theft, provide the police report receipt stating the date, time and place of the theft.

Multi-activity pass

The multi-activity pass is optional and can be added when booking, for ski rentals (alpine or touring) of 5 days or more: €15 per person. It isn't offered with other rentals (snowboard, cross-country, snowshoes, sledge). It lets you swap your gear up to 2 times during the rental, between alpine skiing, snowboarding, cross-country, snowshoes and sledging, to an equal or lower range, subject to availability in the shop. The price of the pass is not refunded.

General rental terms

Article 1. Rented equipment is placed under the customer's responsibility and custody from the moment they take possession of it. The customer is therefore fully responsible for it. Failure to return the rented equipment, for whatever reason, will be invoiced to the customer at its full market value as new, in addition to the rental cost.

Article 2. Equipment is offered for rent in good condition, fit for normal use given the category in which it is classified. It must be returned in good condition. All equipment is numbered or marked and must be returned with the same numbers or marks. Any damage will be charged at the cost of repair, or at the full market value as new if the equipment cannot be repaired, in addition to the rental cost.

Article 3. Rented equipment is for the customer's sole use; it may not be sub-let or lent, even free of charge. The customer alone judges their ability to use the rented equipment.

Article 4. When signing the rental contract, the customer may choose damage and theft cover for an extra charge (see the terms displayed in the shop or on the rental form, "cover" column).

This option exempts the customer, subject to an excess that remains payable by them, from having to pay for damaged or stolen equipment — for theft, on presentation of an official police report. This exemption always carries an excess (see the terms displayed in the shop or on the rental form, "excess" column).

Article 5. The rental period is the one stated on the rental contract; a rental day runs from 8 am to 6 pm.

The rental period may not be changed without the shop's written agreement. The rental price is due from the day the equipment is collected until the day it is returned. Any day started is charged at the publicly displayed prices and terms.

Article 6. Proof of identity may be required before the equipment is handed over.

Article 7. If the rental cost, repair costs or replacement costs are not paid at the end of the contract, the customer, by providing their card details, expressly authorises the shop to debit the amounts still due from their account, in line with the quote (rental contract) they signed when collecting the equipment and/or the prices and terms displayed in the shop.

Article 8. The information requested when you rent helps us know you better. All questions must be answered unless stated as optional. Your information is intended for the rental shop only.

You have the right to access, modify, correct or delete your data (Article 34 of the French Data Protection Act of 6 January 1978). To do so, contact the rental shop.

Article 9. Rentals must be paid in full on the due date. Late payment will incur penalties of one and a half times the legal interest rate.

Article 10. Any dispute relating to this contract will be heard by the court of the defendant's place of residence or of the place where the equipment was made available. Any dispute arising from these general rental terms is governed by French law and the competent French courts. In case of discrepancy, the French version prevails.
